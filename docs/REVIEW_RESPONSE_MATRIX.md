## Research integrity notes

- The benchmark CSVs in this ZIP were generated in the preparation environment with ten repetitions per configuration; they are reproducible example measurements, not portable performance claims. Rerun on the final experiment machine and record its environment.
- The project-source trace is reproducible, but brace depth is only an approximation of scope; this is not compiler-grade parsing.
- `Boost.UnorderedFlatMap` is conditional: if the build machine lacks the Boost header, the output marks that baseline unavailable. The `std::unordered_map` baseline remains available.
- The hybrid is **Adaptive Quadratic-to-Robin-Hood**: triangular-number quadratic probing in Phase 1, then rehash into classical linear-probing Robin Hood. Do not describe it as Robin Hood with per-key double-hashing steps.
